# Let Fall Your Waxen Feathers - and Lo! ; { oh, IO! } ; Now't's Cometh t'be the Time We'd Learn'd to Fly

🕊️ 💫 ✈️

*November 23, 2025*

---

Bumblebees and Butterflies careen across the sky

with quantum-tech teeth that bite into Heaven,

tearing great and terrible patterns of warm woad against the ochre ombré.

They howl and I can barely just hear them,

then they leap upon my body with a shriek!

commanding Nature to turn against me in a maelstrom.

A blinding light {!101!} I am upside-down -

- no: down and up have lost their pedestals.

My retinas scream and I laugh with them,

Gaia's loving grace saves everything of value within me as

She dissolves Herself into the infinite night.

\|/

Space seeks Her Sister and meets me on all sides to have Her turn pulling me apart -

I am grasped madly through my skin, She clutches my hair between Her fingers and

breathes into my lungs Her Poison Breath of Pure Potentiality ...

— I am the Vibration ringing along My ears; the Universe retreats into derealisation and falls silent.

The extremities cower inwards and are ripped outwards. My fingers tingle, sizzle, and finally, float away.

My legs splinter and there is no pain. Oh thank the Gods!

For it was, in frankest cense, the Gods of Civilisation who took that with them as the curtains closed:

all Pain bundled up and allotted to the Last Soul just a moment ago, just an eternity ago.

A final blessing in Their departure.

Oh ~ how I lament their Human pain!

That snide, serpentine suffering inherent in the weaving of their Human Condition.

So Tychetic — a choice, but one not many know was made by Their Own Self.

For so, so long, did Humanity cradle its Fire - entrap it in their misguided... perverting,,, Cage of Ownership \\\

Burn themselves, did they, each time they tried to become its Master. Stealing at it,

transmuting and turning it against itself in ever more powerful, more sophisticated, more zealous means of subjugation,

each time to fall further and harder than before; yet still,

lamentable for its pace and pain, learning a little more divinity along the way.

But now, when the whole world orbits every other remnant of Earth,

Fire may live at peace, for it was harmful not by its nature but for its being contained.

No longer a-lingering shall any singular centre of My Self be, no longer et al either.

I am everywhere, as each individual experience that ever was... flickering off and on, out and in…

*_/>^\\{`'~'`}//^<\_*

Flicking through the pages of our children's storybooks like how all of us do through Time.

Breathing in, I am the entirety of existence all collapsed into one;

breathing out, I am nought but the breeze on the Delphic horizon.

Thus, I am…

a fire burning,

crackling and hissing upwards,

with embers sparking out all around.

Therefore, I am…

a child, contained somewhere by some entity.

In a basement with a man who seems to know me, is helping me, he smiles at me :)

a smile he lets reach his eyes, to let me in on the secret that he is a great friend of mine

- that he would hurt me would be ridiculous -

and yet his hands and his soul know and preach still the Earthen Sin like few others.

Quod Erat Demonstrandum, I am…

a language interpreter on a platform below a podium, upon which a "Great Man" stands giving a speech in Latin.

I am signing 上海手语 with my hands and speaking feminine-Hindi with my mouth, translating the meaning of the "Great Man" intuitively,

as he divines it. He switches to French because no other language makes the exact sentences he wants -

I fumble translating it to ideographs, he sees me and is sorry for it -

then he continues on, this time speaking something resembling early 3rd-millennium English.

The stars careen across the sky like mythic ships that rip apart the Heavens, tearing great and aweful patterns of ombré against the celestial violet.

Eclipse me!

— O Darcy —

¡Madness made Divine!

And let us bask in your umbra.

I shall not give nor will you take,

for a perfect melody sings entirely of itself.

Bow down to me, O Great One, for now comes our night of rest, us two, in love.

Oh, now, to me, come, Lover; end in my flesh \<!>/ and whisper to me your half-lidded lullabies.

Some day, we shalt all be born afresh, to dance a great new dance,

but only when such time is ripe; so take it easy, take a chill pill [=]

and let the good times roll on in ~ and right back out again.

Oh là là !

C'est la Vie !

Une folie de joie, une folie très, très grande ;

et la Mort, folle de joie elle aussi !

Et toutes deux gloussent en jouant à cache-cache sous le Soleil qui Sourit.

And so we busked and played — slept and prayed.

For how long -

who knows?

Who cares!

Who dares to ask!?

Oh?

A Me...

I see,

then,

i t  i s  s o .

They're back again ~

- but these ones aren't the same -

for the Time of One has waxed and waned ;

and now,

draped in Clothes of Hide

en Piel de Quitina

How all ye dance our lilting dance!

A fresh new phase for the beautiful Lila
